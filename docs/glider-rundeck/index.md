# Rundeck Glider Jobs

## Acronyms and Terminology

| Acronym  | Full name                                                                    | Description                                                                                                                                                                                    |
|----------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **DMON** | Digital Acoustic Monitoring instrument (also called **D**igital **MON**itor) | A passive acoustic monitoring instrument developed at WHOI. Used on gliders to record underwater sounds and detect marine mammal vocalizations, including whale calls.                         |
| **DFO**  | Fisheries and Oceans Canada (**D**epartment of **F**isheries and **O**ceans) | The Canadian federal department responsible for fisheries, aquatic ecosystems, and marine science. A partner in glider-based ocean monitoring and research.                                    |
| **WHOI** | **W**oods **H**ole **O**ceanographic **I**nstitution                         | A US oceanographic research institution involved in glider technology and acoustic monitoring.                                                                                                 |
| **WGMS** | **W**ave **G**lider **M**anagement **S**ystem                                | A platform developed by Liquid Robotics for managing Wave Glider missions, monitoring vehicles, and accessing telemetry and sensor data. Its data services support programmatic data retrieval |

- JASCO Glider: A glider associated with JASCO Applied Sciences, a Canadian company specializing in underwater acoustics
  and passive acoustic monitoring, the name originally derived from the name of its founder, Joseph Arnold Scrimger.
